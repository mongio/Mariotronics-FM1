# Mariotronics STOCKHAUSEN Lab 2
An experimental build based on 1.18 for M-VAVE FM-1. The package identity is FM-1_870. The native receiver observes 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi signal strength and traffic from nearby access points. It requires no password, network join, internet connection, or companion computer. The browser displays DEMO and generates synthetic readings.

## Play
Turn ALGORITHM one click past RADIGUE to STOCKHAUSEN. Press EDIT to see the receiver and its four controls. Hold a key. Its pitch is the drone's fundamental. Each note excites a six-mode resonator. A strike sets the body ringing; while the key is held, synthesized impacts and friction excite it again. Traffic increases their density and field strength changes their force. Even in radio silence a held key produces sparse strikes. ENV shapes the final amplitude, including the release.

* Tune 0 scans channels 1–13 and follows the strongest recently seen network. Positions 1–8 choose discovered stations in discovery order and lock to the selected station's channel. Empty positions continue searching. Return to 0 to discover more stations.
* Inertia smooths changes in signal strength. Material morphs from harmonic tube modes to an inharmonic metal plate. Ring adjusts modal decay. Both work while a note is held.
* ARP freezes the last eight seconds of modulation (or the available part). Press again to follow live reception. REC and PLAY operate the shared tape as in 1.18. Switch voice or algorithm to stop the receiver.

Record a loop in FRIPP, switch to STOCKHAUSEN, and hold A while walking a few metres from a nearby 2.4 GHz access point. Observe the signal meter, then compare live and frozen modulation. On the FM-1 a missing factory calibration is shown explicitly. Antenna reception and continuous playback on physical hardware still need testing.

Use the enclosed HTML editor to install this build. The regular Mariotronics 1.18 editor can reinstall the stable firmware. A saved STOCKHAUSEN program recalls the Beating voice in the regular firmware.
