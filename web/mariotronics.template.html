FM-1

Drag a knob vertically or focus it and use the arrow keys. Click the LCD to enlarge it. Play with A W S E D F T G Y H U J K. Z and X shift the octave.

First tape pass

Choose STOCKHAUSEN with ALGORITHM and press EDIT for the four receiver controls. Tune 0 scans, 1–8 selects a discovered station. ARP freezes the recent modulation. In the browser, radio readings are simulated.

Press GLO three times from HOME for ERASE HEAD, or turn SELECT one step left. K1 selects ALL or RANGE. For RANGE, arm K2 and hold REC over the moving tape. Release REC to disarm. In ALL mode hold REC for 1.2 seconds. HOME K1 bends the tape return slightly while changing delay during playback.

Turn PRESETS to recall P01–P64. Press SAVE to open the name editor. K1 moves the cursor, K2 changes the letter, K3 rolls a tape-inspired name. Turn K4 left to erase a letter or right to select Delete Preset. PRESETS chooses the destination. Press SAVE to confirm or HOME to cancel. Tap SEQ to toggle forward/reverse playback and show the decks. Tap REC during overdub for HOLD. Instrument voice is on EDIT, K1.

Tap REC and play C and G. At the default delay, the notes return four seconds later. Tap REC during overdub to enter HOLD and play over the frozen pass. Tap REC again to resume overdubbing. Hold REC for 1.2 seconds to erase the tape. PLAY stops or resumes playback. Tap SEQ again for forward playback.

ALGORITHM selects FRIPP, ENO, RILEY or RADIGUE. RILEY layers sustained Organ, Reed, Reed Organ or Harmonium Drone voices through the shared tape feedback loop and displays the vintage decks. ENO shows a moving signal diagram. ENO offers Piano, Bright Piano, Glass Bell, Tape Choir, Rhodes and Bowed Glass on EDIT K1. Press SEL twice for a six-string fretboard in standard tuning. K1 selects the scale and K2 its root. Gold dots mark the tonic at every octave. Press EDIT repeatedly to move from the voice scope to the active voice controls. Press EDIT again for soundboard response, shimmer, chorus, tremolo, rotary, breath, bellows or slow fade, depending on the active voice. ENO continues through Note FX (note direction, tone, damping and tremolo) and Second Head. ENO and RILEY ARP K1 selects Off, Climb, Fold, Phase or Orbit. Play a single note for a generated figure or hold a chord to sequence its notes. ENO uses wide intervals and adds alternating step lengths in Phase and Orbit. RILEY uses shorter modal intervals and an even pulse. RADIGUE layers slowly beating oscillators over the same tape. Its four drone voices are Beating, Sub, Veil and Fifth. EDIT controls Beat and Color. Each engine remembers its live edits in RAM until another preset is recalled or power is switched off. SAVE writes the active engine to the selected preset. In HOLD, play the other engine over the existing tape. In overdub, its new notes join the recording. In FRIPP EDIT, choose Sing for a rounded, sustained lead or Hollow for a stronger pick transient and more pronounced odd harmonics. EDIT sets drive, tape time and Tape/Loop mode. In Loop mode, tap REC again to set the phrase length, up to eight seconds. Recordings are held in RAM and clear at power-off. Use Power off and Power on to replay the startup animation.

Performance and effects

ENO Acoustic and Bright Piano respond to sustain pedal CC64. Press EDIT twice from HOME for Hammer, Body, Damper and Resonance. Evening Glass, Dusk Choir and Tidal Haze each have four live controls on that same page.

In FRIPP, press ARP twice to reach ARP PICK. Wait delays the first pick and Rise fades in the phrase (0–1000 ms each). Legato connects each pair with one pick. Semi sets the second note 0–24 semitones above a single held key, snapped to the selected scale. Hold two keys for your own interval. SEL TOUCH shapes manual playing with glide, glide time, pick attack and slide curve. Press SEL again for scales.

FX cycles through Off-Tape before Plate. K1 blends the first playback head (180 ms, default 25%) into the monitor during recording and overdubbing. LFO opens vibrato rate (Hz), depth (cents), wait and rise (0–1000 ms each). Wait sets how long a fresh note stays steady. Rise sets how gradually vibrato reaches full depth. Legato slides carry the vibrato forward. Press LFO again on its page to switch vibrato on or off. In FRIPP, ARP switches two-note picking on and off. Hold one key for root and fifth, or hold two different notes to alternate them in the order played, with a repeating 3 + 3 + 2 accent. One held note plays normally. Set speed (3–16 notes per second), gate and accent on the ARP page. SEL TOUCH controls manual articulation. Press SEL again for Scales. Choose Chromatic, Dorian or Major and a root. For the modal centres of The Heavenly Music Corporation, try F-sharp Dorian, A major and D major. Eric Tamm’s analysis.

FX cycles through Compressor, Fuzz, Wah, Amp/Cab, EQ, Filter, Off-Tape and Plate. The first six shape the instrument before recording. The stereo plate follows the sum of the live instrument and tape return, so its tail stays out of the recording. ENV opens attack, decay, sustain and release for both engines. HOME knob 1 adjusts Tape Delay (4.0–8.0 seconds).

Global MIDI and Bluetooth

Press GLO once from HOME for TAPE CHARACTER: HF LOSS, SATURATION, WOW and FLUTTER. These global settings apply across presets. The second GLO page is TAPE SPEED. K1 changes the speed of the recorded loop. K2 selects STEP (4.75, 9.5, 19 or 38 cm/s) or VAR for intermediate rates. The reference is 19 cm/s. The third page is ERASE HEAD, followed by MIDI, BLE MIDI and LIGHTS. K1 sets idle key brightness to OFF, HALF or FULL. Held notes light at maximum brightness. The fourth GLO page sets the receive channel (Omni or 1–16), CC input, external sync and delay in quarter-note beats (1–16). With sync enabled, Tape follows a 24-PPQN MIDI clock up to eight seconds. Start rewinds and plays an existing tape or begins recording an empty one. Continue resumes; Stop halts the transport. With no clock the manual tape time takes over. Loop mode retains its recorded length.

Note On and Off include velocity and channel filtering. Program Change 0–63 recalls P01–P64. CC 1 controls vibrato depth, 7 amp volume, 11 tape send, 20 fuzz drive, 21 wah sweep, 22 feedback, 23 tape return and 91 plate mix. CC 120 and 123 release incoming notes on their channel.

On the FM-1, press GLO again for BLE MIDI. Turn K1 on. With Device at 0, press OCT+ to scan for ten seconds. Use K2 to select a discovered controller, then OCT+ to connect. USB MIDI and the TRS MIDI input also receive notes, controllers and clock. The FM-1 keyboard transmits notes over USB and the connected BLE link, on the selected channel (channel 1 in Omni). In the emulator, enable MIDI below and select a system input and, optionally, an output. For Bluetooth, pair your controller in the operating system first.

Time Lag Accumulator · Terry Riley

In Paris in 1963, Riley worked with a technician on a two-recorder system for Music for The Gift. The second machine’s playback signal returns to the first, where it mixes with the live input. At constant tape speed, increasing the distance between the heads lengthens the delay. In Tape mode, turn HOME knob 1 while playing. Between four and eight seconds, the A77s move progressively apart.

Frippertronics · the 1979 setup

The Les Paul feeds the pedals and the left Revox. Tape crosses the recording head, reaches the playback head of the second A77 and winds onto the right reel. An audio cable returns the second machine’s output to the first. At 19 cm/s, 76 cm between heads gives a four-second delay.

FM-1 firmware and recovery

Connect one powered FM-1 directly by USB in desktop Chrome or Edge. Close other MIDI editors. Installation replaces the instrument firmware. Keep this tab open, the computer awake and the cable connected until the new identity is verified.

Preset backup

Export all 64 saved slots, names and stored patch settings. Live tape, unsaved edits, global settings and firmware are excluded. USB preset transfer requires Mariotronics 1.4 through 1.7 or version 1.18 on the FM-1. Choose the source or destination below. Power off the simulator before transferring its bank.

Interrupted update or black screen
  1. If the update stopped, reconnect USB and select Identify and install. An FM-1 update loader can finish the interrupted transfer.
  2. If the loader is missing, power on while holding OCT− to start USB recovery, then identify and install again.
  3. If the computer identifies WL80UBOOT1.00 (USB 4C4A:8057), download and extract the GRANA rescue kit on a Linux PC or Raspberry Pi. Read README-EN.txt. The kit reads the entire flash twice and saves a copy before offering to restore GRANA. After GRANA boots, install Mariotronics here.
  4. If the computer detects no USB device, check the data cable, power and port before choosing a recovery procedure.
